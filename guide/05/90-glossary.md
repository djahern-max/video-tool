# Glossary

Reference material. Nothing here is required reading, and none of it counts
toward the course's word count (7.02.5).

Each definition traces to the OpenAI and CPA.com documents listed as this
lesson's sources, or says that it cannot. Three terms — *context window*,
*GPT Instant / GPT Reasoning*, and *input maximum* — are row labels on
OpenAI's pricing grid, which gives figures for each and defines none.

---

**Context window**
A row of OpenAI's pricing grid, given for each plan and each of two
models as a number followed by K; the captured grid ranges from 27K to
256K. The grid does not say what the figure counts or what K stands for.
(OpenAI, "Pricing", personal and business tabs.) *Section 06.*

**Examples (in a prompt)**
Sample answers supplied with a request. The CPA.com toolkit says that
providing the model with examples helps get a better output. (CPA.com,
"Generative AI Toolkit.") *Section 03.*

**File uploads**
A row of OpenAI's pricing grid. Included on ChatGPT Business; on the
personal tab, limited on Free and included from Go up. What "limited"
means is not stated in the grid. (OpenAI, "Pricing", personal and
business tabs.) *Section 06.*

**GPT Instant / GPT Reasoning**
The two model names under which OpenAI's pricing grid gives a context
window and an input maximum for each plan. The grid gives figures for
each and describes neither. (OpenAI, "Pricing", personal and business
tabs.) *Section 06.*

**Input maximum**
A row of OpenAI's pricing grid, given in pages of text for each plan and
each model: about 12 pages for the Instant model on Free, about 40 on
Business, about 320 for the Reasoning model on both business plans, and
about 680 on Pro. The row points to a footnote that is not in the
captured page. (OpenAI, "Pricing", personal and business tabs.) *Section
06.*

**Iterative refinement**
OpenAI's practice of starting with an initial prompt, reviewing the
response, and refining the prompt based on the output. (OpenAI Help
Center, "Prompt engineering best practices for ChatGPT.") *Section 04.*

**Prompt**
A text input that initiates a conversation or triggers a response from
the model; it can also take other forms, such as an image or audio.
(OpenAI Help Center, "Prompt engineering best practices for ChatGPT.")
*Section 01.*

**Prompt engineering**
The process of designing and optimizing input prompts to effectively
guide a language model's responses. (OpenAI Help Center, "Prompt
engineering best practices for ChatGPT.") *Section 01.*

**Tone**
Set in a prompt with descriptive adjectives; OpenAI's page lists formal,
informal, friendly, professional, humorous, and serious as words that
help guide the model. (OpenAI Help Center, "Prompt engineering best
practices for ChatGPT.") *Section 03.*

**Verbatim excerpt**
A passage returned in the document's own words rather than the model's.
The CPA.com toolkit says that prompting the model to return verbatim
excerpts mimics the way a human researcher gathers information in order
to reach conclusions. (CPA.com, "Generative AI Toolkit.") *Section 05.*
