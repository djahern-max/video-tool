# How much can go in

<!-- index: 6#2, 6#3, 6#4, 6#11; 7#2, 7#3, 7#4, 7#9 -->

A prompt has a size limit, and the limit is not one number. It depends on
the plan and on which model answers, and OpenAI's pricing page is where
the figures are.

## Two figures per model

The pricing page's comparison grid gives, for each plan and for each of
two models it calls GPT Instant and GPT Reasoning, a "total context
window" and an "input maximum". The input maximum is given in pages of
text; the context window is given as a number followed by K, which the
grid does not explain. The input maximum is the figure this section is
about, because it is the one stated in the unit a CPA works in.

## The Instant model

On the personal tab, the Instant model's total context window is 27K on
Free and 54K on Go; on the business tab it is 54K on Business and 128K on
Enterprise. The input maximum for the same model is about 12 pages of
text on Free and about 40 pages on Business. The same model, then,
accepts more than three times as much in one request on a Business plan
as on a Free one.

## The Reasoning model

Both business plans give the Reasoning model a 256K context window and an
input maximum of about 320 pages of text. On the personal tab, the Pro
plan's Reasoning input maximum is about 680 pages, the largest figure on
either tab. Within one plan, then, the two models differ by a factor of
several; within one model, the plans differ again.

## What the figures mean for a task

Two things follow. The first is that the bound is a property of the plan
and the model, not of the task: the same document may fit in one request
on one plan and not on another, and may fit with one model and not with
the other. A CPA who does not know which plan and model are in use does
not know the bound. The second is that the figures are the page's, the
page states no date for them, and the footnote its input-maximum row
points to is not something this lesson can give.

## Files

Whether a document goes in as pasted text or as a file is a separate row
of the same grid. File uploads are included on Business; on the personal
tab they are limited on Free and included from Go up. What "limited"
means is not something the grid says. Section 05's practice of attaching
a document and asking for excerpts from it depends on this row as much as
on the input maximum.
