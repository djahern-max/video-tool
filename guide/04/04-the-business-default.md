# The business default and its one exception

<!-- index: 10#1, 10#2, 10#3, 10#9, 10#10, 10#23; 12#7, 12#1 -->

Section 01 stated the business default. This section says where it comes
from and what the one exception is.

## The default

OpenAI's enterprise privacy page is OpenAI's statement of its commitments
for business data, which it defines as inputs and outputs from ChatGPT
Business, ChatGPT Enterprise, ChatGPT for Healthcare, ChatGPT Edu,
ChatGPT for Teachers, and its API Platform. The first of those
commitments is on training: by default, OpenAI does not use your business
data to train its models. The page repeats it product by product at the
end: data from each of those products, and from the API Platform after
March 1, 2023, is not used for training by default. The help-center page
on how your data is used says the same for business users, naming ChatGPT
Business, ChatGPT Enterprise, and the API.

## The exception

The default has one exception, and the enterprise privacy page states it
in the same paragraph: if you have explicitly opted in to share your data
with OpenAI, for example through its opt-in feedback mechanisms, to
improve its services, then OpenAI may use the shared data to train its
models. Three words carry the weight. *Explicitly*: the default does not
change on its own. *Opted in*: the direction is the reverse of the
individual plan's, where the user must opt out. *Feedback mechanisms*:
the page's own example of an opt-in is the same kind of thing that
section 03 described on the individual side, a feedback control that
puts data into training. Who in a workspace can give that opt-in is not
something the page states.

## Review without training

Not training on business data is not the same as never looking at it,
and the page is specific about the difference. It says OpenAI may run any
business data submitted to its services through automated content
classifiers and safety tools, including to better understand how its
services are used, and that the classifications created are metadata
about the business data and do not contain the business data itself. It
says business data is subject to human review only as the page describes,
service by service. What that description says for ChatGPT Business and
for ChatGPT Enterprise is in section 05.
