# Turning training off on an individual plan

<!-- index: 8#1, 8#2, 8#4, 8#8, 8#9; 12#3; 10#4 -->

The setting that changes an individual plan's training default lives in a
part of ChatGPT that OpenAI's help center calls Data Controls. The Data
Controls FAQ says that these settings let you decide how ChatGPT uses your
conversations and interactions, and specifically whether your
conversations help improve OpenAI's models. That second sentence is the
training control, and it is the subject of this section and the next.

## Where it is

There are two places to turn training off, and either one is enough. The
first is inside ChatGPT: under Settings, then Data Controls, the setting
is a switch labelled "Improve the model for everyone", and turning it off
stops your conversations from being used to train ChatGPT. The second is
outside it: OpenAI's privacy portal offers an option worded "Do not train
on my content". The page on how your data is used says that for ChatGPT,
either option is sufficient, and that you do not need to opt out in both
places.

## What it covers

Once training is turned off, the setting applies to the entire account;
it does not matter which device you are using. The switch is set once,
not once per phone or laptop. It can also be changed at any time; the FAQ
says there are no restrictions.

## Business plans

The FAQ answers a separate question about business plans in one sentence:
OpenAI's Team, Enterprise, and Edu plans offer additional data controls,
and for those it refers the reader to OpenAI's enterprise privacy page.
That page describes the two plans it names for deploying ChatGPT to
employees as ChatGPT Business and ChatGPT Enterprise, and this lesson uses
the names that page uses. The switch described in this section is the
individual plan's; what a workspace gives its admins instead is the
subject of sections 05 and 06.
