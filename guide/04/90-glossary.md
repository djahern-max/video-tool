# Glossary

Reference material. Nothing here is required reading, and none of it counts
toward the course's word count (7.02.5).

Each definition traces to the OpenAI documents listed as this lesson's
sources, or says that it cannot. One document — the print-layout capture
of OpenAI's enterprise privacy page — is listed as a supporting source
because it supplies the page's one-line description of its SOC 2 audit,
which the Reader-view capture the body relies on does not carry.

---

**Business data**
OpenAI's term, on its enterprise privacy page, for inputs and outputs from
ChatGPT Business, ChatGPT Enterprise, ChatGPT for Healthcare, ChatGPT Edu,
ChatGPT for Teachers, and its API Platform. The page's commitments,
including the training default, attach to it. (OpenAI, "Enterprise privacy
at OpenAI.") *Sections 01 and 04.*

**Data Controls**
The settings in ChatGPT that OpenAI's help center says let you decide how
ChatGPT uses your conversations and interactions, and specifically whether
your conversations help improve OpenAI's models. (OpenAI Help Center, "Data
Controls FAQ.") *Section 02.*

**Data Processing Addendum (DPA)**
An addendum OpenAI says it is able to execute with customers for their use
of ChatGPT Business, ChatGPT Enterprise, and the API. (OpenAI, "Enterprise
privacy at OpenAI.") *Section 06.*

**Enterprise Compliance API**
The route through which, on ChatGPT Enterprise, workspace admins can
access an audit log of conversations and GPTs. (OpenAI, "Enterprise privacy
at OpenAI.") *Section 05.*

**"Improve the model for everyone"**
The label on the training switch under Settings → Data Controls on an
individual ChatGPT plan. Turning it off stops your conversations from
being used to train ChatGPT; they still appear in your chat history.
(OpenAI Help Center, "Data Controls FAQ.") *Sections 02 and 03.*

**Privacy portal**
The second place OpenAI provides for opting out of training on an
individual plan, by selecting "Do not train on my content"; either it or
the switch in ChatGPT is sufficient on its own. (OpenAI Help Center, "Data
Controls FAQ"; "How your data is used to improve model performance.")
*Section 02.*

**SOC 2 Type 2**
An audit that OpenAI's enterprise privacy page says ChatGPT Business and
ChatGPT Enterprise have each successfully completed. The same page
describes its SOC 2 audit as confirming that its controls align with
industry standards for security and confidentiality. (OpenAI, "Enterprise
privacy at OpenAI", both captures.) *Section 06.*

**Temporary Chat**
A chat that OpenAI says will not appear in history, will not use or create
memories, and will not be used to train its models; it is deleted from
OpenAI's systems after 30 days and may be reviewed only to monitor for
abuse. (OpenAI Help Center, "How your data is used to improve model
performance"; "Data Controls FAQ.") *Section 03.*

**Training (on conversations)**
OpenAI's word for the use of conversations to improve its models: its help
center says ChatGPT improves by further training on the conversations
people have with it, unless you opt out. What training does with a
conversation technically is not described on either help-center page.
(OpenAI Help Center, "How your data is used to improve model
performance.") *Section 01.*

**Workspace admin**
The role that OpenAI's enterprise privacy page gives control over a
ChatGPT Business or ChatGPT Enterprise workspace: on Business, able to
view, access, export, and delete end user conversations; on Enterprise,
able to access an audit log of conversations and GPTs; on both, in control
of how long data is retained. The page uses the term without defining it.
(OpenAI, "Enterprise privacy at OpenAI.") *Sections 05 and 06.*
