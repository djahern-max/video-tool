# Two kinds of service

<!-- index: 12#1, 12#2, 12#7; 10#1, 10#2, 10#3, 10#4, 10#22, 10#23 -->

This lesson is about setting ChatGPT up so that a firm's work goes into it
on terms the firm has chosen. It begins with the one distinction
everything else in the lesson depends on: whether an account is one of
OpenAI's services for individuals or one of its services for businesses.
The two sides start from opposite defaults on training.

## Services for individuals

OpenAI's help-center page on how your data is used to improve model
performance says that ChatGPT improves by further training on the
conversations people have with it, unless the user opts out. When you use
OpenAI's services for individuals, which the page names as ChatGPT and
Codex, OpenAI may use your content to train its models. OpenAI's
enterprise privacy page says the same from the other direction: among the
data OpenAI trains on is data from the versions of ChatGPT and other
services for individuals. An individual plan, then, trains on
conversations by default, and the default holds until the user changes
it. Sections 02 and 03 are about how to change it and what the change
does and does not do.

## Services for businesses

For businesses, the same help-center page states the opposite default: by
default, OpenAI does not train on any inputs or outputs from its products
for business users, including ChatGPT Business, ChatGPT Enterprise, and
the API. OpenAI's enterprise privacy page says the same in its own words,
twice: by default, business data is not used for training; and by
default, data from ChatGPT Business, ChatGPT Enterprise, ChatGPT for
Healthcare, ChatGPT Edu, ChatGPT for Teachers, and the API Platform after
March 1, 2023 is not used for training. That page defines "business
data" as inputs and outputs from those products, and its commitments
attach to that. Of the products it names, two are the ones it describes
as the way to deploy ChatGPT to a firm's employees: ChatGPT Business and
ChatGPT Enterprise. This lesson uses those two names throughout, and
sections 04 to 06 are about them.

## The line, stated once

On an individual plan, conversations are used for training unless the
user opts out. On ChatGPT Business or ChatGPT Enterprise, they are not,
unless the customer has explicitly opted in; section 04 says what that
opt-in is. Which side of the line an account is on is a fact about the
account, not about the person using it: a CPA who signs in to a personal
ChatGPT account at work is on the individual side, whatever the work is.
