# What the setting does and does not do

<!-- index: 8#3, 8#5, 8#6; 12#4, 12#5, 12#6, 12#8 -->

Turning off "Improve the model for everyone" changes one thing, and it
helps to be exact about what.

## It is prospective

The page on how your data is used says that after you opt out, OpenAI
will not train its models on your new conversations. The word is *new*.
Neither page says what happens to conversations already used for training
before the switch was turned off, so the switch is best read as governing
what happens from now on.

## It does not clear history

The Data Controls FAQ says that after training is turned off, your
conversations will still appear in your chat history but will not be used
to train ChatGPT. The switch is about training, not storage: the
conversation stays where it was.

## Feedback can override it

The page on how your data is used adds a case that a careful user needs
to know. Even after opting out, you can still give feedback on a
response, for instance by selecting thumbs up or thumbs down; and if you
do, the entire conversation associated with that feedback may be used to
train OpenAI's models. A single click on a rating can put a whole
conversation back into training on an account that has opted out.

## Temporary Chat

Both pages describe Temporary Chat as a different case. The page on how
your data is used says that chats from Temporary Chat will not appear in
history, will not use or create memories, and will not be used to train
OpenAI's models. The Data Controls FAQ adds two limits: Temporary Chats
are deleted from OpenAI's systems after 30 days, and they may be reviewed
only to monitor for abuse. A Temporary Chat is therefore not the same as
an opted-out account: it leaves no history to return to, and it is still
on OpenAI's systems for up to 30 days.

## What stays with OpenAI

Whatever the setting, the page on how your data is used says that OpenAI
retains certain data from your interactions, and that it takes steps to
reduce the amount of personal information in its training datasets
before they are used. What is kept, and for how long, on an individual
plan is not something either page states. Lesson 5 takes up what that
means for client information.
