# Who can see a workspace's conversations

<!-- index: 10#4, 10#11, 10#12, 10#13, 10#16, 10#17, 10#18 -->

The enterprise privacy page names ChatGPT Business and ChatGPT Enterprise
as the two ways to deploy ChatGPT to a firm's employees, and it describes
each. ChatGPT Business is built for small and growing businesses and
offers collaborative tools and self-serve access to ChatGPT in a dedicated
workspace for a team. ChatGPT Enterprise is built for businesses and
offers the controls, deployment tools, and speed that the page says are
required to make an entire organization productive. The page's answers
about who can see conversations differ between the two, and this section
keeps them apart.

## ChatGPT Business

On ChatGPT Business, the page says that end users can view their own
conversations, and that workspace admins have control over workspaces and
can view, access, export, and delete end user conversations in the
workspace. For a firm, that is the sentence to read twice: an admin of
the firm's workspace can read a staff member's chats. OpenAI's own access
to conversations stored on its systems is limited, the page says, to
authorized employees who need it for engineering support, investigating
potential platform abuse, and legal compliance, and to specialized
third-party contractors bound by confidentiality and security
obligations, solely to review for abuse and misuse.

## ChatGPT Enterprise

On ChatGPT Enterprise, end users can again view their own conversations;
the organization has control over workspaces, and workspace admins can
access an audit log of conversations and GPTs through what the page calls
the Enterprise Compliance API. Authorized OpenAI employees, the page says,
will only ever access your conversations to resolve incidents, to recover
an end user's conversations with your explicit permission, or where
required by applicable law.

## Reading the two together

On both plans, then, a workspace member's conversations are reachable by
the workspace's admins, and OpenAI's own access is stated as a limited
list. The page describes the admin's route differently for the two plans:
view, access, export, and delete on Business; an audit log through the
Enterprise Compliance API on Enterprise. Nothing in this section is a
reason to prefer one plan over the other. It is what a firm agreeing to
either should know its own admins can do, and lesson 5 returns to it.
