Practical Expedients." The `.txt` Codification files are paragraphs copied
verbatim; the ASU `.txt` files are text extractions of the PDFs beside them
(pypdf, layout not preserved — page headers and line breaks intrude, so quote
from the PDF when exact wording matters). Only files marked **authoritative**
may support a claim in a lesson; the other two are context.

## Note on `842-20-30-3.txt`

The file's content is the short-term lease **reassessment** guidance ("...the
lease no longer meets the definition of a short-term lease and the lessee
shall apply the remainder of the guidance in this Topic as if the date of the
change in circumstances is the commencement date"). It is **not** paragraph
842-20-30-3: ASU 2021-09 (in this directory) amends 842-20-30-3 and quotes it
in full — it is the discount-rate paragraph carrying the risk-free rate
election. The reassessment text matches what current-feature.md calls
842-20-25-3. A reviewer with Codification access should confirm the paragraph
number and rename the file; until then the text itself is treated as verbatim
Codification of unconfirmed number.
